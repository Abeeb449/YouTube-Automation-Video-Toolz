Have you ever wondered why some people seem to get so much more done in a day? The secret is not working longer hours. It is building a system that works for you. [visual: pexels:busy office desk]

# Step one: capture everything

Your brain is for having ideas, not holding them. Write every task, idea and promise into one trusted inbox. A notebook works. So does a notes app. The tool matters far less than the habit of using it every single time something comes up.

# Step two: decide what matters

Each evening, pick the three most important tasks for tomorrow. Not ten. Three. When you wake up, you already know where your energy should go, and you avoid wasting the best hours of your morning deciding.

# Wrapping up

Capture everything, then choose three priorities a day. That is the whole system. Try it for one week and tell me in the comments how it went.
